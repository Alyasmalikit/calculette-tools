How Duration Addition & Subtraction Works

This calculator converts both time durations into total seconds, adds or subtracts them, and converts the result back into clean hours, minutes, and seconds—even if you enter more than 60 minutes or 60 seconds.

Practical Uses

Use it to combine task timers, add video or audio clip lengths, or subtract elapsed time from a total budget of hours and minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the second duration is larger during subtraction? The result is shown in absolute duration values without breaking.

Can I enter minutes above 59? Yes. Any value over 59 is automatically rolled over into hours.
