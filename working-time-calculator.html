Total Working Time: 8 hours 0 minutes (8.00 decimal hours)

How to Use This Working Time Calculator

Enter your shift start time, shift end time, and total break time in minutes. If your end time is earlier than your start time (for example, 22:00 to 06:00), the calculator automatically treats it as an overnight shift across midnight.

Formula & Example

Net Working Time = (End Time - Start Time) - Break Minutes. For example, working from 09:00 to 17:30 is 8 hours 30 minutes; subtracting a 30-minute break gives 8 hours 0 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support night shifts? Yes. When end time is earlier than start time, 24 hours are added to the end time automatically.

How are decimal hours calculated? Minutes are divided by 60 (for example, 30 minutes = 0.50 hours).
