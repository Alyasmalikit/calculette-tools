Why Track Total Working Hours?

Tracking total working hours helps freelancers, hourly employees, and managers plan schedules, spot overtime early, and estimate billing or payroll accurately.

Example Calculation

If you work 8 hours per day for 6 days, your total is 48 hours. With a regular limit of 40 hours, you have 40 regular hours and 8 overtime hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use decimal hours like 7.5? Yes. Enter decimal values such as 7.5 or 6.25 directly into the hours per day field.

Is pay calculation mandatory? No. Leave the hourly rate at 0 if you only need total and overtime hours.
